Hey, I'm **3ch0**. I'm 18, based in the UAE, and I take IoT devices apart to find out how they really work. Firmware, debug ports, locked bootloaders: if a vendor hoped nobody would look there, that's where I start.

I'm just as hooked on AI and cybersecurity, and curious about nearly everything in between. Most recently I spent 13 to 14 November at the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026** at the University of Dubai, where my team built [Lumasphere](https://github.com/3ch0less/spaceappsproject).
